MacroLab brings official macroeconomic data and econometric models into your browser.

Start with the existing Same ECB shock, pooled template. Here, Germany, France, Italy and Spain face the same identified monetary policy shock.

Run the analysis, then focus on two outcomes: real GDP and the price level. Use the existing country selector to compare Germany, France, Italy and Spain in turn, then switch between the GDP and price responses.

The horizontal axis shows quarters after the shock. Each line shows an estimated cumulative response, while the shaded bands show uncertainty. A tightening shock need not produce identical responses across countries, and a point estimate alone is not a firm conclusion.

These are conditional estimates. The shock is constructed using the template’s existing recursive identification, rather than a directly observed policy surprise. The bands do not include uncertainty from estimating that shock, and they do not establish statistically significant differences between countries.

Use the charts to explore a question, inspect the assumptions, and export the results. MacroLab runs the calculation locally, with no account or coding required.
