Deneysel / Experimental: known limits

  • A Found label does not validate the question's premise. Direction, permanence, targeting and variable/mechanism claims can escape the checks.
  • Multiple dates, report periods and publication dates may be resolved incompletely. Verify the period on the official source.
  • Relevant evidence may remain only under Related; weak word overlap can miss an existing source.
  • Selected extracts are not complete reports. Rationale, timing, eligibility and other conditions may be absent. Missing evidence does not establish that an event did not occur.
  • The tool returns original quotations, not a generated economic explanation or a reliable automated fact-check. Check the date, instrument, actor and qualifiers yourself.

Validation scope

The expensive 160,958-field numerical regression evidence is retained with source and payload hash checks; it is not presented as a fresh full run. A fresh run of the existing ECB template supplies the demo and case. Release-specific checks cover the presentation, links, historical share configurations and a reproducible clean build.

These three measurements are archived research evidence, not a guarantee of public-network response times or a new live-answer benchmark.
